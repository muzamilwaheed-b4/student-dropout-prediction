Project Documentation & Technical Notes

Experimental design, methodology, and implementation breakdown.

1. Dataset Source & Characteristics

This study utilizes the Predict Students' Dropout and Academic Success dataset published on the UCI Machine Learning Repository (Realinho et al., 2021). The records originate from a higher education institution and cover diverse undergraduate programs.

  • Total Instances: 4,424 observations
  • Predictor Variables: 36 attributes covering demographic characteristics, socio-economic factors, entrance examination metrics, and academic progress across semesters 1 and 2.
  • Target Classes: Dropout (32.1%), Enrolled (18.0%), Graduate (49.9%).
2. Data Cleaning & Feature Preprocessing

To maintain strict methodology and prevent data leakage:

  • Partitioning: An 80/20 stratified train-test split was performed prior to applying scaling and synthetic resampling.
  • Outlier Treatment: Capping was applied to extreme continuous entrance and course grades using the standard IQR rule (Q1 - 1.5*IQR to Q3 + 1.5*IQR).
  • Standardization: Feature scaling via StandardScaler was fit exclusively on training data and subsequently applied to test vectors.
  • Class Imbalance Mitigation: SMOTE was executed only on the training feature matrix to balance minority class samples.
